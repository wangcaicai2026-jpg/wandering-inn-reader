CHAPTER 01 · PART 01

The Empty Inn

空荡荡的旅店 · A1 改写版
阅读进度 0%

The inn was dark and empty. It stood on one of the highest hills. The grass moved in the wind. Some grass was green, some was orange, and some was purple.

There were big mountains far away. Their tops were in the clouds. She could see no other buildings from there. The old inn was the only building she could see.

Long ago, people came to this inn. They ate, drank, and slept here. But the inn had been empty for many years. The windows were dark. The rooms were full of dust.

One evening, a young woman ran toward the inn. Her name was Erin Solstice. She ran through the grass. She was very tired. She was afraid, too.

Her right arm was burned. Her legs hurt. She looked behind her again and again. There were monsters outside. She had seen a Dragon. Small green monsters had chased her, too.

Erin did not know where she was. She did not know how to go home. Then she saw the inn. Maybe someone was inside. Maybe someone could help her.

She walked up the hill. She stopped at the door. "Hello?" she called. "Is anyone here?" Nobody answered. The sky was getting dark.

Erin did not want to stay outside. The monsters might come back. She opened the door. "Hello? I need—" She stopped. The inn was empty.

Dust was everywhere. The tables and chairs were old and dirty. Erin began to cough. "Oh, come on!" "Of course it is empty."

She wanted to cry. Her arm hurt. Her legs hurt, too. "It is not fair," she said. "Everything is going wrong."

The inn was big and dark. Erin was alone. She was afraid to go in. But she was afraid of the monsters outside, too. "I just wanted to go to the bathroom," she said.

Then she heard a sound above her. She ran outside and felt cold raindrops. Then the rain became very heavy. Erin ran inside. The rain was loud.

Maybe the Dragon did not like rain. Maybe the green monsters could not find her. Maybe she was safe now. Erin found an old chair. It was lying on its side.

She stood the chair up. She cleaned it a little. Then she sat down. She closed her eyes. She wanted to rest.

But then she opened one eye. She looked around the empty inn. "I am really hungry," she said.

Erin sat in the old inn. She looked around. Where am I? she wondered. A short time ago, she had been at home.

She played chess in her room. Then she wanted to go to the bathroom. She walked toward the bathroom. But there was no bathroom. There was a cave. And in the cave, there was a Dragon!

Erin stood up. Oh no! It was real! The Dragon was very big. It had purple fire. The purple fire seemed to miss her. But she still felt its heat. The heat burned her arm.

Erin looked at her arm. It still hurt. This is not a dream, she said. She remembered more. After the Dragon, she ran outside. She wanted help. Help! Help! she shouted.

But nobody came. Then she saw some small green monsters. They had red eyes. And many teeth. They ran after her. Erin ran as fast as she could. The monsters hurt her legs. But she got away.

Maybe they were Goblins? she said. She did not know. Erin looked around the inn again. There were old tables. There were old chairs. There was no plastic or glass in sight. There were no electric lights. Everything seemed to come from another time. The inn looked like a place from long ago.

Is this a magic world? she asked. Do I need a sword? She thought about it. A sword would be good. But right now, she needed something else. The inn was very dirty. There was dust everywhere.

Erin started to cough. She could not stop. Oh, come on! This place is so dirty! Someone needs to clean this place. She looked around. Nobody came. Erin sighed. Oh. That someone is me.

Erin looked at the rain outside. She was afraid, but she tried to stay calm. Do not scream, she told herself. Something might hear me. She sat at a table and tried to think. First, she needed a safe place. The inn had walls and a roof.

It was dirty, but it was better than the rain. She could stay here for now. She could think about what to do next. But first, she had to clean. Erin stood up and checked her pockets. She had nothing useful. There was no phone, no food, and no bag.

She had only the clothes she was wearing. I was just going to the bathroom, she said. How did I get here? She looked around the large room. Dust covered every table and chair. She walked behind the bar. There were some dusty mugs, but no cleaning cloth.

She found a door behind the bar and opened it. Behind the door was a kitchen. There were cupboards, a stone stove, and an old oven. She saw a pot and a pan. Then she saw something useful. A bucket! Erin picked it up and smiled.

She carried the bucket outside. Rainwater quickly filled it. She also put some cups outside to catch more water. Now she had water, but she needed a cloth. She opened the cupboards one by one. She found plates and old kitchen tools inside. There was no cloth.

She searched the rest of the ground floor. Still nothing. Only one place was left. Upstairs. Erin looked at the dark stairs. She did not want to go up. But she needed that cloth.

Erin put one foot on the first stair. The old wooden stair creaked loudly. She stopped and listened. Nothing moved. Come on, Erin, she whispered. You can do this. She took another step.

The stairs creaked again. Her heart beat faster. What if there were monsters upstairs? Maybe there were Goblins. Maybe there was even a Dragon. But how could a Dragon fit upstairs? At least, she hoped not.

Erin gave a small, nervous laugh. She knew there were probably no monsters upstairs. But the darkness and the unknown still scared her. Dustrag, she whispered to herself. Dustrag, dustrag, dustrag. She said the words again and again. Step by step, she reached the second floor.

The hall was very dark. She could barely see a few steps ahead. She entered the first dark room. Then she heard a strange sound. Something made a soft rustling sound. Erin froze and listened. Was there a monster in the room?

A strong wind blew against the inn. A white thing suddenly flew toward her. Erin screamed and fell to the floor. The thing covered her face and arms. She tried to push it away. She threw the thing away and stood up. The monster was not a monster at all.

It was a curtain. Erin looked at the old cloth in her hands. Curtains, she said, still in shock. Her heart was still beating fast. It was dirty, but she could use it. She closed the window and went downstairs. She put the curtain next to the bucket.

Now she could start cleaning.

Erin looked at the dirty tables. Where should she start? She decided to clean the table near her. She put the curtain into the bucket of water. Then she pulled it across the table. A cloud of dust rose into the air. Erin coughed and stepped back.

The dust made her cough, but cleaning was easy. She wiped the table again. This time, the wet cloth took the dust away. Soon, she could see the brown wood. The water in the bucket turned grey. But the rain gave her plenty of fresh water. Erin threw the dirty water away.

She filled the bucket again. Then she cleaned another table. And another. After some time, all the tables were clean. Erin looked at the chairs. Well, I can clean those too. She cleaned the chairs one by one.

Then she cleaned the long bar. The wood looked beautiful without the dust. For the first time, the inn felt a little warmer. Erin looked at the clean wood. But the floor was still very dirty. All the dust from the tables was on the floor now. She had no mop.

She looked at the floor and laughed. Clean tables, dirty floor. At least the tables are clean, she thought. She looked outside. The rain had stopped. Night had come. The inn was almost completely dark.

Erin felt afraid again. But the clean tables made the room feel different. She had cleaned the room herself. It almost felt like her own place. She sat down and closed her eyes. She was very tired. Maybe tomorrow would be better.

Maybe it was all just a dream. As she fell asleep, she thought about food. Now I am really, really hungry.

Erin closed her eyes. She was very, very hungry. Soon, she was almost asleep. Then strange words came to her in the darkness. Innkeeper Class Obtained! Innkeeper Level 1! Skill: Basic Cleaning obtained!

Skill: Basic Cooking obtained! Erin opened one eye. She looked around the dark room. Then she sat up. What was that?
